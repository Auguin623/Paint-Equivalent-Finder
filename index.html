Color Matching Explained

Match quality is measured by Delta E, the perceptual distance between two colors. You can switch between two algorithms using the toggle in the header:

CIE76 (Fast) Simple, quick calculation. Great for general browsing. Works well for most colors.
CIEDE2000 (Accurate) Advanced formula that better matches how your eyes see color. Best for tricky shades like blues and purples.

Match badges tell you how close a match is at a glance:

● Excellent ● Good ● Fair
